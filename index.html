Export any web table to Excel, CSV or your clipboard. Pay once. No account. Nothing leaves your computer.

How it works

  1. Open a page with a table and click the SheetGrab button.
  2. SheetGrab lists every table on the page. Hover to see which is which.
  3. Copy it, save it as CSV, or save it as an Excel file.

Why people choose it

  • Pay once. Other table exporters charge a yearly subscription for the same features.
  • Private by design. Everything runs in your browser. The only network call is checking a Pro licence key.
  • Minimal permissions. SheetGrab touches only the page you click on.
  • Merged cells and hidden rows handled. Column and row spans land in the right cells; hidden rows are left out unless you ask.
