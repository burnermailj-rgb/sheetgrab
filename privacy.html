SheetGrab privacy policy

Last updated 16 October 2026.

What SheetGrab does

SheetGrab is a browser extension that exports tables from the web page you are looking at to Excel, CSV or your clipboard. All processing happens inside your browser.

What data it reads

When you open SheetGrab on a page, it reads the tables on that page in order to export them. It does this only on the page where you clicked the SheetGrab button, and only while the popup is open. It does not read pages in the background, and it does not read anything on pages where you have not opened it.

What leaves your computer

Nothing from the pages you export. Table contents, file names and your settings stay on your device.

The only network request SheetGrab makes is to check a Pro licence key, if you enter one. That request goes to the payment provider Lemon Squeezy (api.lemonsqueezy.com) and contains the licence key and a random install identifier. Lemon Squeezy's handling of that request is described in its privacy policy.

What is stored

Your settings, and your licence key with its activation status if you bought Pro, are stored in the browser's local extension storage on your device. A multi-page capture in progress is kept in session storage and cleared when the browser closes. SheetGrab sets no cookies and uses no analytics, no tracking and no advertising.

Purchases

Pro is sold through Lemon Squeezy, which acts as the merchant of record and handles payment, receipts, VAT and refunds. SheetGrab never sees your payment details.

Permissions, in plain words

  • Read the page you clicked on (activeTab, scripting): needed to find and export its tables.
  • Storage: to remember your settings and licence.
  • Downloads: to save the exported files.
  • api.lemonsqueezy.com: to check a Pro licence key.
